The article argues that exceptional product makers succeed by becoming users of their own products, creating tight feedback loops that enable intuitive decision-making. This internal evaluation capability, rather than external validation cycles, generates the flow state that makes building addictive. AI amplifies this advantage by accelerating the build process, but only benefits makers who already possess strong self-evaluation skills.
